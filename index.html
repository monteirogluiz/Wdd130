Santa Catarina

Santa Catarina is a state in southern Brazil known for its beautiful beaches, mountains, nature, and diverse culture. The state also has a strong European cultural influence and is home to Beto Carrero World, the largest theme park in Latin America. Santa Catarina is also known for its delicious food, including seafood, barbecue, polenta, and traditional Italian dishes.
